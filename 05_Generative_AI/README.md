# Project 5: AuraGen Creative Multimodal GenAI Studio

A Generative AI platform that synthesizes rich narrative storyboards, visual prompts, and procedural abstract concept art in customizable aesthetic styles.

---

## 📌 Features
- **Narrative Storyboard Generator**: Synthesizes creative story lore, character premises, and visual direction prompts.
- **Procedural Visual Canvas**: Generates high-resolution concept art images matching aesthetic styles (Cyberpunk Neon, Fantasy Mystic, Sci-Fi Nebula, Impressionist Sunset, Minimalist Architectural).
- **Export & Download**: One-click download of generated concept art as PNG.
